# Stage 5 — Full study, release, and paper

**Labels:** `tracking`, `stage-5`, `release`, `paper`, `ui`

Direction: [docs/DIRECTION.md](../docs/DIRECTION.md) §2 and §3.

## Scope
- [ ] Full runs: every arm, at least 3 models, at least 3 seeds, on SEC-bench, ARVO/T2L-ARVO, and VulnLoc. Paired statistics with confidence intervals.
- [ ] Strata: crash-to-root-cause distance, reuse present or absent (the epoch ablation), and before or after each model's training cutoff.
- [ ] Cost: tokens, USD, wall time, replay and query latency; an accuracy-against-cost Pareto plot.
- [ ] Release:
  - [ ] `statesolver` CLI and MCP server: Debian package, install script, Crumb marketplace package, signed releases. The rr dependency and PMU requirement must be documented.
  - [ ] Listings on elixpo.com and research.elixpo.com.
- [ ] Optional: a visual UI (timeline, allocation lifetimes, evidence graph) over the same API.
- [ ] Paper: every number regenerated by one command; artifact with annotations, transcripts, and the checker.
- [ ] arXiv: cs.SE primary, cross-listed to cs.AI and cs.CR. Venues: ICSE, FSE, ISSTA, or USENIX Security.
- [ ] Before submission:
  - [ ] the maintainer's Google Scholar check (papers citing WATCHER, Ravel, and DebugHarness);
  - [ ] a re-check of arXiv for 2026 agent-debugging work.
